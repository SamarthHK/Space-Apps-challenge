Scientific Integrity Note: Satellite vs Laboratory Soil Testing
Satellite remote sensing (Sentinel-2, Landsat) measures canopy optical reflectance, leaf chlorophyll, and surface moisture index. Satellites cannot directly measure underground chemical nutrient levels like Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), or Potassium (K). Those values must always come from physical Soil Health Cards or laboratory soil tests. Our engine uses laboratory data for nutrient calculations and satellite imagery for real-time vegetative monitoring.

Understanding Your Expected Farm-Gate Price

A farmer rarely receives the highest quoted APMC price on the news. To calculate the real cash in hand, our engine deducts APMC cess (1.5%), mandi weighing & bagging charges (~₹80/qtl), and truck/tempo freight transport from farm-gate (~₹4.50/qtl/km). This protects you against overestimating revenues before committing funds.
